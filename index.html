Explore the Philippines

The Philippines is home to some of the most stunning natural wonders in Southeast Asia. From white sand beaches to breathtaking mountains, here are five beautiful destinations worth discovering.

Bucas Grande Island

This island is packed with numerous caves and white sand beaches, waterfalls, mangroves, iron woods, inland lakes, fishing grounds, and corals.

Puerto Princesa Underground River

Located in Palawan, the Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park features a stunning limestone karst landscape with a navigable underground river that flows directly into the sea.

Baguio Cathedral

The Baguio Cathedral is most famous for its distinct rose-colored exterior, twin square belfries, and its history as a safe haven during World War II

Mayon Volcano

Situated in Albay, Bicol, Mayon Volcano is renowned for its near-perfect symmetrical cone shape. It is an active stratovolcano that dominates the landscape of the region.

Siargao

Siargao is a teardrop-shaped island in the Philippine Sea known for its world-class surf breaks such as Cloud 9. It also features crystal-clear lagoons and lush coconut palm forests.
